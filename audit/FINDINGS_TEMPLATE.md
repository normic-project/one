## Description

State the violated property and the concrete behavior. Separate observed facts from assumptions.

## Impact

Describe assets, users, markets, authority or availability affected; include maximum credible loss or lock where possible.

## Preconditions and attack path

List required access, state, timing, liquidity, transaction ordering and external dependency behavior. Provide a minimal reproduction or test.

## Recommendation

Describe the smallest safe correction and any migration/operational consequence. Do not modify the frozen candidate in place.
